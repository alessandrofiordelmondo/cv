## About me

Alessandro Fiordelmondo is an electronic music performer and researcher in Time-based Media Art and Computer Music.
He studied at the Padova Conservatory of Music, where he received his master's degree with honours in Electronic Music Composition. He studied with Nicola Bernardini, Alberto Novello, and Alvise Vidolin, among others.
Between 2022 and 2024, he carried out a PhD in the History of Art, Film, Audiovisual Media and Music course at the Department of Humanities and Cultural Heritage Studies (DIUM), University of Udine. He was supervised by Cosetta Saba and Sergio Canazza, and his main research topic was the reactivation and preservation of new multimedia art forms.
Since 2019, he has been a research assistant at the Centro di Sonologia Computazionale (CSC), the computer music laboratory of the University of Padua, and Audio Innova, a spinoff of the same university. He has worked on audio/video performances and production, reactivation of multimedia installations, and multimedia software development.
In 2022, he was appointed an expert in Computer Engineering for Music and Multimedia at the Department of Information Engineering (DEI), University of Padua.
He has presented his works and performed at various national and international festivals, including MUSLAB in Mexico City, the SMC—Sound and Music Computing Conference in Stockholm, the Musiques Démesurées festival in Clermont-Ferrand, the Greek National Opera's Sacred Music Festival in Athens, the Biennale Architettura and Biennale Arte in Venice, the CIM—Colloquio di Informatica Musicale in Udine, Sagra Malatestiana in Rimini, and SEAMUS in West Lafayette (Indiana, USA).

## Repository info

This project generates the curriculum vitae in both PDF and Markdown from [`cv.json`](cv.json).

### Generate the CV

```bash
python3 -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python3 main.py
```

The command updates `output/main.pdf`, `output/main.tex`, and this README. The PDF and README are rendered from the same prepared data.
